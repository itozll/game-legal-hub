# Meow Garden Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Effective date: October 7, 2026

## 1 Scope and developer

This policy explains information handling in Meow Garden and third-party services involved when you visit this privacy policy website. We are the developer of Meow Garden. Developer name: gaoguodong. Privacy contact email: joiky@163.com.

The game runs through a host application or platform runtime. The host application's own accounts, permissions, advertising and other data processing are also governed by its privacy policy. This policy does not replace the policies of the host application or advertising providers, or remove our responsibility for our own processing.

## 2 Information handled by the game and its purposes

The current game code does not provide account registration, sign-in, cloud saves or payments. It does not actively collect nicknames, profile pictures, gender, phone numbers, government identification numbers, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone data. It does not integrate a separate behavioral analytics or crash-reporting service.

To provide game features, we store the following in your device's storage or local storage made available to the game by its host:

- Game progress: the current level, completed levels, daily challenge completion dates, stars and remaining hints, to restore progress and record rewards.
- Current game state: board markings, placed cats, remaining lives, game mode, puzzle identifiers and elapsed time, to resume an unfinished game.
- Game settings: sound, animation and language preferences, to retain your choices.

On first launch, the game reads the system language setting to choose an interface language. You can change the language in game settings. Daily challenges use the UTC date supplied by your device to select a puzzle locally, without sending the date or game progress to our servers.

The current implementation stores progress and preferences locally. We do not operate a server to receive these saves, and the game does not actively send saves to advertising interfaces. Local storage is not anonymization; someone else with access to the device or host storage may be able to see game records.

## 3 Third-party runtime and advertising

When the host supplies the SUD OpenPaaS runtime, the game uses its interstitial and rewarded video advertising interfaces. Interstitial advertisements may appear between levels. Rewarded videos provide hints or a one-time revival reward. You can choose not to watch a rewarded video, which affects access to that video reward.

The game passes an advertising placement identifier when creating an advertisement, and handles load, error and close events in memory. Playback completion status determines whether a reward is granted. These interfaces are not used by the game to actively submit your local game saves.

The host application, runtime and actual advertising providers may independently process advertising requests and device or network-related information. The specific data categories, purposes, retention periods, processing locations, lawful bases and privacy choices depend on the disclosures of the host application and advertising providers you actually use. This project has not independently verified the data processing practices of every host version and advertising supply chain.

Platform entry: [SUD OpenPaaS](https://openpaas.sud.tech/). The platform's published policy entry: [SUD Privacy Policy](https://www.sud.tech/privacy). That entry does not replace the specific disclosures required from the actual host or advertising provider.

Where applicable law requires consent for third-party processing, personalized advertising or tracking, valid consent must be obtained before that processing starts. Choosing to watch a rewarded video does not constitute consent to personalized advertising or cross-application tracking. You can use privacy controls supplied by the host and your device to manage relevant permissions. The game currently has no separate personalized advertising switch.

## 4 Storage locations and retention

Game progress, current state and preferences are stored on your device or in local storage supplied by the host. They usually remain until you clear the relevant data, uninstall the application or the host removes the game's storage. The actual outcome depends on the platform's backup, restore and uninstall behavior. When loading a save, the game retains at most the latest 366 daily challenge completion records.

We do not promise that local saves are stored in a particular country, and we do not describe third-party storage locations as the developer's local storage location. The host, advertising services and website host may process information outside your country. Their policies and actual services determine the relevant locations, retention periods and safeguards for international transfers.

If you contact the developer, we use the contact information and issue details you provide only to handle your request, and retain them for the period necessary to address it and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not require government identification numbers or information unrelated to your request.

## 5 Use and disclosure

We use the information above only to operate the game, retain progress and preferences, handle advertising rewards and respond to your requests. The current game implementation does not upload local saves to developer servers or provide a developer-operated feature for selling local saves or publicly disclosing game records.

Third-party advertising and hosting processing are described in sections 3 and 8. If we introduce collection, sharing, transfer or public disclosure of information, we will update this policy, provide the notices required by applicable law and obtain your valid consent where required. Disclosures required by law will be limited to what is necessary.

## 6 Your choices and rights

You can change language, sound and animation preferences in game settings. To delete local progress, use your browser's site data controls or a game data removal feature supplied by the host. Clearing game data may also remove progress, rewards and preferences. The game currently has no cloud recovery. Refer to your platform's instructions for the actual effect of uninstalling or clearing data.

Where local law applies, you may request access, a copy, correction or deletion of personal information; restriction of or objection to processing; withdrawal of consent; or raise a complaint. Contact us using the email in section 10. We will handle requests under applicable law and verify identity only where necessary. We cannot remotely inspect or delete saves that exist only on your device and have not been provided to us, but we can explain how to manage them locally.

For information independently processed by the host, advertising services or GitHub, you can also contact the relevant provider directly and complain to the competent data protection authority.

## 7 Protection of minors

Minors should use the game with guidance from a parent or guardian and follow the age requirements of the host application and their jurisdiction. The current game does not actively collect age information and has no registration or identity verification feature. The game should not be understood as having completed every jurisdiction's child-specific advertising and data protection configuration.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent, we must obtain it before the relevant processing. Use of the game alone must not be treated as that consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have processed information about your child that should not have been processed, contact us using section 10. We will investigate and take deletion or other necessary steps under applicable law.

## 8 Privacy policy website

This static policy website does not include analytics scripts, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, external fonts or cookies used to record reading activity. Visiting it does not start game advertisements or read game saves. The common entry point uses the language supplied in the URL or the browser language settings only within the browser to select the corresponding policy page. It does not save language preferences or send them to developer servers.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when they visit GitHub Pages sites. GitHub is responsible for that hosting processing. See [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
